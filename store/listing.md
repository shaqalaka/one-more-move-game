## Full description

A quiet circuit. A limited number of turns. One final spark.

One More Move is a focused portrait puzzle built for satisfying 30–90 second sessions. Rotate tiles to create a continuous energy path from the source to the beacon before your moves run out.

**Every puzzle is solvable**  
Boards are generated from a known valid route and provide enough moves to restore it. There are no timers, hidden rules, or random events after play begins.

**A shared Daily Circuit**  
Play the same date-seeded challenge every day, build a local streak, and share your result without revealing the solution.

**One last chance**  
When your moves reach zero, the Last Spark gives you exactly one final rotation. Make it count.

**Designed for calm play**

- Fast one-thumb sessions
- Offline gameplay
- Immediate retries
- Optional hints and undo
- Reduced-motion support
- Keyboard and screen-reader labels
- Generated sound effects with a mute control
- No account, ads, analytics, or tracking in version 1

Your scores and settings remain on your device. Reset them any time from the menu.
