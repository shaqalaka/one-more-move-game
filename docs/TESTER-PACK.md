# Closed-Test Recruitment Pack

Google's production-access gate for a new personal Play account generally requires at least 12 testers to remain opted into the closed test continuously for 14 days. Recruit 15–20 dependable testers so one dropout does not reset progress. Verify the exact requirement displayed in the account.

## Invitation draft

**Subject:** Can you test a new one-minute puzzle game for 14 days?

I am preparing a small Android puzzle called **One More Move**. Each round takes roughly 30–90 seconds: rotate tiles to connect an energy path before the move counter reaches zero.

Google requires a closed test before a new developer can request public access. I need Android testers who can:

1. Join using the official Google Play opt-in link.
2. Remain opted in for at least 14 continuous days.
3. Install the test build on an Android device.
4. Play several rounds across the period and send honest feedback.

There is no account inside the game, advertising, analytics, or purchase. Progress remains on the device. Joining is voluntary and you can leave, but please tell me first because falling below Google's tester threshold may restart the qualification period.

## Day-one instructions

- Install only from the official Play closed-test link.
- Complete the tutorial.
- Play at least five random circuits.
- Try the Daily Circuit.
- Test mute, hint, undo, restart, background/resume, and Reset progress.
- Report crashes, unclear rules, impossible-looking boards, accidental rotations, clipped controls, or audio problems.

## Follow-up schedule

- Day 1: installation and first-session check
- Day 4: short gameplay reminder and first feedback
- Day 8: Daily Circuit and return-play check
- Day 13: confirm testers remain opted in
- Day 15: final feedback and thank-you after the continuous window is safely complete

Do not incentivize positive reviews or ask closed testers to post public ratings. Rewarding testing time, if done, must not depend on sentiment, review content, or a store rating.

## Feedback questions

1. What did you think the objective was after the tutorial?
2. Did the powered path and disconnected path look clearly different?
3. Did any board feel impossible? If so, capture a screenshot before restarting.
4. How many rounds did you play voluntarily?
5. Did you use Hint, Undo, or Last Spark?
6. Did any tap rotate the wrong tile or register twice?
7. Were any controls clipped or hard to reach?
8. Did sound, mute, resume, or reset behave unexpectedly?
9. Would you play tomorrow's Daily Circuit without a reminder?
10. What single change would most improve the game?

Include device model and Android version with technical reports.

## Success gates before production

- No reproducible crash or progress-loss defect
- Tutorial objective understood by at least 90% of testers
- No generated board shown to violate the canonical solvability test
- At least half of testers voluntarily play more than three rounds
- At least one-third return on another day without troubleshooting pressure
- No unexpected network traffic, permission, privacy, or billing behavior
- Play pre-launch report has no unresolved high-severity issue
